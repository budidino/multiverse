Privacy Policy

Dino Budimilic built the Norwegian 4x4 app as a commercial app with optional subscriptions and a one time lifetime purchase.

Norwegian 4x4 does not collect, store, or process any personal data on its private servers. All data is stored locally and privately on user's device or iCloud.

The app works fully offline. No account is required and no personal information is requested or saved.

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Norwegian 4x4 does not have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or any personal data related to purchases.

The app may use anonymous and aggregated analytics to understand feature usage and improve the app experience. This data cannot be used to identify you.
